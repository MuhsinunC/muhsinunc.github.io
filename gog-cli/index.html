gog CLI (personal)

gog CLI (personal) is a private command-line tool that one person, the owner of this site, runs on his own computer. It lets him work with his own Google account from the terminal:

  • create, read and edit his own Google Docs and Google Sheets;
  • find, upload, download and organize files in his own Google Drive;
  • read and change events on his own Google Calendar;
  • look up his own Google Contacts (read-only).

The tool is not offered to the public. No one else signs in to it, and it has no website accounts, no servers and no users other than its owner.

It is built on the open-source gogcli command-line client.
